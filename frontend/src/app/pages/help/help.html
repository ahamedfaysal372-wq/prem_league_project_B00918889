Help & Support

Find answers to the most common questions

Getting Started

How do I create an account?

Click the Join button in the top right corner of the navbar. Fill in your name, email address and a password of at least 6 characters. Once registered you can log in immediately.

How do I log in?

Click the Log In button in the top right corner of the navbar. Enter your registered email address and password and click Log In.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Please contact our support team at support@premierleague.app and we will help you reset your password as soon as possible.

Using the App

How do I view the league table?

The league table is displayed on the Home page. It is automatically updated whenever a match result is recorded. Click on any team row to view their full profile.

How do I find a specific player?

Go to the Players page. You can search by name using the search box or filter by position using the dropdown menu. Results are paginated so use the Next and Prev buttons to browse.

How do I view match results?

Go to the Matches page. You can filter matches by status — select Finished to see completed results or Scheduled to see upcoming fixtures.

How do I view a team's details?

Go to the Teams page and click on any team card. You will be taken to the team's detail page showing their stadium, manager and full season statistics.
